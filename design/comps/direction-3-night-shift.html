Case study / fork in the road

Rebuilding Fork In The Road: 113,000 lines became 18,000 that do more

Fork In The Road helps groups decide where to eat. Version one worked but had grown to 113k lines, 27 runtime dependencies, and 77 API routes nobody could hold in their head. The constraint for version two: keep every shipped feature, cut everything else, and let guests vote securely without accounts.

role: solo, end to end 9 phases, one PR each live: forkintheroad.app

Sign the ballot, not the guest

Each guest gets an HMAC-signed httpOnly cookie holding zero personal data. Revotes replace the ballot in place; signing up later claims history via a one-way pointer.

Tradeoff: clear cookies, lose your vote. Better than an account wall that kills group adoption.

Kill the cron

Expiry is lazy: any read seals an expired round, and the SSE stream doubles as the timer. Crash-safe because the seal derives from timestamps, not from a job having run.

Tradeoff: a round nobody reads seals late. Nobody is waiting on it.

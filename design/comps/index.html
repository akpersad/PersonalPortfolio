Phase 2: design direction pick sheet

Three full directions plus four avatar treatments, all built from the Design Engineering Manual (tinted OKLCH neutrals, one accent, type paired on a contrast axis, motion 150-300ms ease-out, both themes first-class). Open each file, toggle the theme, and resize to 360 / 768 / 1280. Every direction uses the same real copy and the same Fork In The Road case study so the comparison is apples to apples.

Direction 1

Annotated

The engineer's drafting table. Slate-tinted paper, signal-orange markup, mono annotations, dimension lines, ledger rows. Bricolage Grotesque + Geist + Geist Mono.

Argument for: wears the design-engineer positioning literally; the annotation motif scales to case studies and the colophon. Safest strong choice.

 Direction 2

Ink & Margin

The published engineer. Warm-gray paper, oxblood ink, optical-size serif, Tufte-style margin notes, numbered table of contents. Newsreader + Instrument Sans + JetBrains Mono.

Argument for: the strongest frame for writing-first authority (the leerob/rauno lesson) and the most distinctive at lead level. Requires the copy to stay excellent.

 Direction 3

Night Shift

Dark-first, and the theme toggle IS the story: enterprise by day, products by night. Slate surfaces that elevate by lightness, one ember accent. Clash Display + Geist.

Argument for: the only direction with a narrative hook built into the interface. Risk: dark product portfolios are a crowded genre; the hook is what earns it.

 Deliverable B

Avatar treatments

One master likeness redrawn from the reference photos, rendered four ways: flat, mono-line, duotone print, sticker. Shown under all three palettes in both themes.

Note: pick a treatment, not a final. The chosen one gets a likeness refinement pass before export.

How to decide

  1. Squint test each hero: does the hierarchy survive?
  2. Read the case study section in each: which frame makes the FITR story land hardest?
  3. Toggle dark mode: which identity survives both themes without losing itself?
  4. Pick one direction and one avatar treatment (mixing is fine: any avatar works in any direction).
